You Feed Your Stomach Every Day… But What About Your Mind?

Aug 10, 2025

Let’s face it — most of us don’t think twice before spending money on things that vanish in minutes:

  • ₹300 for a movie ticket ("Chill yaar, weekend hai!")
  • ₹250 on Zomato/Swiggy ("Bas ek treat de deta hoon apne aap ko")
  • ₹100 for chai-samosa with friends ("Thoda gossip bhi ho jayega")

But when it comes to investing in English, the one skill that could decide whether you get your dream job or not… suddenly, we hesitate.

1. English = Job Opportunities You’re Currently Missing

Many freshers lose jobs not because they lack technical skills — but because they can’t express themselves confidently in English during interviews. That 10–15 minutes of awkwardness can cost you months of job hunting.

Fact: Recruiters often shortlist candidates based on how well they speak, not just what they know.

A samosa fills your stomach. English fills your resume.

2. You’re Already Spending More Without Realising

Let’s do quick math:

  • 1 chai-samosa: ₹40–₹50 × 20 days = ₹800–₹1000/month
  • 1 Swiggy/Zomato order: ₹300 × 4 times a month = ₹1200/month
  • 1 movie: ₹300 × 2 = ₹600/month

That’s ₹3000+ gone every month — with nothing to show for it after a week.

Our English learning course costs less than that and stays with you for a lifetime.

3. The World Doesn’t Wait for Bad English

Whether you’re a fresher or a final-year student, opportunities don’t pause until your English improves. If you don’t start now, you’ll still be in the same place next year while others move ahead.

English opens doors to:

  • International clients
  • Better-paying jobs
  • Networking with professionals worldwide

4. Confidence = Money in the Bank

Imagine this: Two candidates have the same qualifications.

  • Candidate A speaks fluently and confidently in English.
  • Candidate B stammers, searches for words, and mixes grammar.

Guess who gets hired? Guess who gets promoted faster?

Spoiler: It’s not about who’s smarter — it’s about who communicates better.

5. English Improves Everything Else Too

When your English is strong:

  • You understand lectures, books, and tutorials better.
  • You can apply for internships abroad.
  • You can crack exams like IELTS, TOEFL, GRE.
  • You impress in presentations, group discussions, and client calls.

A pizza is tasty. English is profitable.

6. Skills Have ROI. Food Doesn’t.

A ₹500 burger is gone in 10 minutes — zero return on investment.

A ₹500 English class can help you land a ₹5 lakh/year job — huge return on investment.

It’s not about “spending” money. It’s about multiplying it.

Final Words: Samosa for the Stomach, English for the Future

If you can spend on things that make you happy for an hour, you can definitely spend on something that makes you successful for life.

🎯 Don’t wait. Every day you delay learning English is an opportunity missed.
