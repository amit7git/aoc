LSRW Daily Practice Guide

Follow these recommendations to master Spoken English through consistent daily practice

🎧 Listening Activities

Goal: Improve comprehension, vocabulary, and pronunciation

✅ Your Mentor's Recommendation:

Watch English news, favorite shows

💡 More Recommendations:

  • 🎧 Podcasts: BBC Learning English, The English We Speak
  • 🎬 Videos: YouTube interviews, TED Talks
  • 🎶 Songs: Listen English Songs with Lyrics
  • 👂 Audiobooks: Use Audible or LibriVox

📝 Daily Reflection:

➤ What new words or phrases did you hear today?

🗣️ Speaking Activities

Goal: Improve fluency, clarity, and confidence

✅ Your Mentor's Recommendation:

Tell what you've done in English (morning to evening), practice class activities

💡 More Recommendations:

  • 🪞 Mirror Practice: Speak for 5 mins daily
  • 👥 Video Diary: Record 1-2 min about your day
  • 🗣️ Conversation: Speak with friend or relatives
  • 🎲 Prompts: Use conversation starters

📝 Daily Reflection:

➤ What was difficult to say? Which sentence did you say well?

📖 Reading Activities

Goal: Build vocabulary, grammar sense, and comprehension

✅ Your Mentor's Recommendation:

Use Inshorts app to read news aloud and clearly

💡 More Recommendations:

  • 📚 Stories: Read graded English readers
  • 📲 News Apps: BBC News, Medium, Quora
  • 🔎 Read Aloud: Practice pronunciation
  • ✅ Speed Reading: Skimming and scanning

📝 Daily Reflection:

➤ What article did you read? What new words did you learn?

✍️ Writing Activities

Goal: Improve grammar, sentence structure, and expression

✅ Your Mentor's Recommendation:

Write what you've learned today

💡 More Recommendations:

  • 📝 Daily Journal: Write 5-10 sentences
  • ✍️ Writing Prompts: Describe favorite place
  • 🗒️ Summarize: Write about what you read
  • 💡 Vocabulary: Write sentences with new words

📝 Daily Reflection:

➤ Write 3-5 sentences using today's new words

🌟 Keys to Success

✅ Be Consistent
Practice all 4 skills daily

✅ Track Progress
Use this Tracker regularly

✅ Focus on Growth
Progress over perfection

✅ Stay Engaged
Choose topics you enjoy

📖 How to Use This Tracker

  • 1. Daily Practice: Complete all 4 skills (LSRW) each day
  • 2. Rate Confidence: Select your confidence level from dropdown
  • 3. Write Journal: Add optional notes about your experience
  • 4. Mark Complete: Click when all 4 skills are rated
  • 5. Track Progress: Check Progress and Analytics tabs
  • 6. Celebrate: Enjoy milestone achievements!

💡 Success Tips:

• Practice at the same time daily
• Use headphones for listening
• Record yourself speaking
• Review journal entries weekly
• Don't skip days - stay consistent!
