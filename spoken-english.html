Course Overview

Our Spoken English course is designed to improve your fluency and communication skills. We focus on practical, real-world application, helping you build the confidence to speak English naturally and effectively in professional meetings, interviews, and daily life.

  • 🌍 Anyone looking to improve their overall fluency from beginner to advanced levels.

What You'll Learn

Our curriculum is structured to build your skills logically, from mindset and grammar to advanced fluency.

Module 0 The Foundation for Success (Mindset & Orientation)
  • Welcome & Course Structure: What to expect in our 1-on-1 sessions.
  • The 'Why' & 'How': The importance of English and the ALEXA coaching method.
  • Mindset Mastery: Overcoming fear, hesitation, and nervousness.
  • Techniques: How to start thinking directly in English.
  • First Steps: How to introduce yourself confidently.
Module 1 English Grammar - The Blueprint of the Language
  • The Absolute Basics: Parts of Speech, Sentence Structure, Subject-Verb Agreement.
  • Verbs & Tenses: Mastering the most used tenses (Simple Present, Past, Future, etc.).
  • Adding Nuance: Using Modals (can, could, should) for politeness and possibility.
  • Advanced Structures: Conditionals, Active/Passive Voice, and Degrees of Comparison.
Module 2 Vocabulary & Pronunciation - Sounding Natural
  • Vocabulary Building: Strategies to learn and retain new words effectively.
  • Core Vocabulary: Mastering the 1000 most-used real-life words.
  • Pronunciation & Accuracy: Correcting common mistakes and using spoken contractions.
  • Word Formation: Understanding Prefixes, Suffixes, and Root Words.
Module 3 Functional English - Mastering Everyday Conversations
  • Essential Conversational Structures: Asking questions, making requests, expressing opinions.
  • Situational English: Role-playing for shopping, doctor visits, directions, etc.
  • Daily Interactions: Mastering small talk, discussing hobbies, and daily routines.
  • Practical Skills: Asking for and telling the time, expressing likes/dislikes.
Module 4 Advanced Communication & Fluency Building
  • Advanced Vocabulary: Idioms, Proverbs, Phrasal Verbs, and Collocations.
  • Fluency Techniques: Practicing spontaneous speaking and descriptive skills.
  • Professional English: Phrases for meetings, emails, and job interviews.
  • Body Language Training: Developing confident non-verbal communication skills.
  • Modern English: Understanding current slang and social media trends.
