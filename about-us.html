Our story

A simpler way to learn English

ALEXA Online Classes was founded by Amit, an English mentor with more than seven years of teaching experience.

The name ALEXA stands for Amit Linguistic English Xperience Arena. It was created around a simple belief: learners do not need more information as much as they need guidance, practice and a comfortable space to use English.

That is why classes are live, questions are welcome, and lessons connect grammar, vocabulary and speaking to situations learners actually face.

How we teach

A practical, personal approach

Every class is designed to move from understanding to using English, with a mentor close enough to notice what each learner needs.

Live, two-way classes

Sessions happen on Google Meet, so learners can ask questions, speak up and get guidance in the moment. There are no pre-recorded lessons standing in for a mentor.

Personal attention

Choose one-to-one learning or a small group of up to three. The setting stays focused enough for every learner to take part.

Practice for real situations

Grammar, vocabulary and speaking are connected to work, interviews, exams, travel and everyday conversations, so progress is easier to use.

Confidence through clarity

Explanations are kept clear, mistakes are treated as part of learning, and feedback is specific enough to act on.

What guides us

Our mission and vision

01

Our mission

To make English learning smarter, easier and more practical, so every learner can speak with confidence. We want to unlock opportunities, strengthen self-belief and help people connect across cultures without language holding them back.

02

Our vision

A world where communication has no boundaries. We see ALEXA as a place where learners become confident speakers who can take part in education, work and life beyond their home town with clarity and self-assurance.
