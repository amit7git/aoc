Frequently asked questions

What is ALEXA Online Classes?

ALEXA Online Classes is an online platform offering free and paid English courses like Grammar, Spoken English, Vocabulary etc. through live Google Meet sessions.

How do I join ALEXA Online Classes?

Visit alexaonlineclasses.vercel.app/demo
Step 1: Book a free demo class.

Step 2: Attend the demo class.

Step 3: Fill out the registration form shared after the demo class.

Step 4: Join the class based on your selected plan.

Who teaches the classes?

We have a team of expert English mentors who guide you throughout your learning journey.

Are the classes really free?

Our free classes are available on weekends exclusively for registered learners. Please book a free demo class first to get your Registration ID.

What are ALEXA Prime Classes?

Prime Classes are our paid classes that include daily live sessions, class notes, assignments, and 1-on-1 doubt sessions.

What is the difference between Free and Paid classes?

Free classes: Weekly, no notes/assignments.
Paid classes: Daily, notes, assignments, permanent Google Meet link.

How do I book free weekend classes?

You can book your free weekend class in advance by following the steps here. Visit alexaonlineclasses.vercel.app/free-class

Is there a registration fee?

No, registration is completely free for all learners.

What courses are offered?

✔ English Grammar – Tenses, Articles, Sentence Structure
✔ Spoken English – Conversations, fluency
✔ Vocabulary – Idioms, phrasal verbs, word usage.
Check Available Courses here alexaonlineclasses.vercel.app/courses

Do I need to use my video during class?

No, video is optional. Audio participation is enough.

Can I cancel or reschedule my class?

Yes, just inform us in advance to reschedule or cancel.

How do I join a class?

Free classes: Link sent to your email before the session.
Paid classes: Permanent Google Meet link provided.

How do I pay for Prime classes?

Pay after each class using UPI methods like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm etc.

Can I move from Free to Paid classes later?

Yes, you can upgrade anytime without a new registration.
We follow a one-student, one-registration system. You can enroll in all courses with a single registration.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes, classes are designed for beginners and intermediate learners.

Are the classes live or recorded?

All sessions are live for real-time interaction. No recordings are provided.

Do I get class notes or recordings?

Prime or paid students receive notes. No recordings are provided.

What do I need for class?

✔ A willingness to learn
✔ A stable internet connection
✔ A device with Google Meet access
✔ A quiet learning space
✔ An Earphone or a Headphone
