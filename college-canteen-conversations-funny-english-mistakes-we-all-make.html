College Canteen Conversations: Funny English Mistakes We All Make

Aug 10, 2025

The college canteen — that magical place where chai flows, samosas vanish in seconds, and English grammar goes on vacation! Whether you’re with your gang or impressing your crush, you’re bound to drop a few hilarious Hinglish lines. Let’s check out the funniest blunders that turn our canteen gossip into a comedy show.

1. “I Am Taking Tea Since Half an Hour” – Tea is Patient, But Not This Much!

You’re sipping chai and casually announce, “I am taking tea since half an hour.” Bro, are you taking it hostage?
Funny Example: “I am taking tea since half an hour.” Friend: “Wah, tea is lucky to have such a loyal owner!”
Fix: “I have been drinking tea for half an hour.” Grammar bhi garam chai ki tarah correct hona chahiye!

2. “Please Pass Me One Plate Samosa” – Like It’s a New Currency

Ordering snacks and you say, “Please pass me one plate samosa.” Arre, plate bhi aur samosa bhi singular?
Funny Example: “Please pass me one plate samosa.” Friend: “Are you planning to eat it or frame it?”
Fix: “Please pass me a plate of samosas.”

3. “Today I Am Feeling Boring” – Poor You, Poor Listeners!

You tell everyone, “Today I am feeling boring.” Bhai, tum nahi, tumhara sentence boring hai!
Funny Example: “Today I am feeling boring.” Friend: “Good, then you match the lecture in the morning!”
Fix: “Today I am feeling bored.”

4. “I Didn’t Had Breakfast” – Time Travel Alert!

While complaining about hunger, you say, “I didn’t had breakfast.” Are you from the future?
Funny Example: “I didn’t had breakfast.” Friend: “Kya tum kal ka bhi breakfast skip kar ke aaye ho?”
Fix: “I didn’t have breakfast.”

5. “Give Me a Cold Water” – Water Isn’t an Exam Paper

You confidently ask, “Give me a cold water.” Bhai, ‘a’ kyun? Kitna ekdum special hai yeh paani?
Funny Example: “Give me a cold water.” Friend: “Do you also ask for ‘a rice’?”
Fix: “Give me some cold water.”

6. “I Am Eating My Lunch Daily” – Commitment Goals!

Someone asks about your routine and you proudly say, “I am eating my lunch daily.” As if it’s breaking news!
Funny Example: “I am eating my lunch daily.” Friend: “Wow, such discipline… India should give you a medal.”
Fix: “I eat my lunch daily.”

Conclusion: Canteen English is the Best Comedy Show

So, next time you’re in the canteen, keep your chai hot, your samosas crispy, and your English even crispier. Laugh at your mistakes, fix them, and remember — life is too short to skip chai or correct grammar in silence.

Learning English alone is like tea without sugar. Share it with friends for the full flavour!
