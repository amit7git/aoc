Terms and conditions for Prime classes

Please read these before you book.

Enrollment and payment

Registration is completed on the website. Payment for Prime classes is collected only after the class has been attended.

Attendance and cancellation

To avoid being charged, please cancel your scheduled class at least 2 hours before the start time.

Rescheduling

You may reschedule your class up to 2 hours in advance. New session slots are subject to availability.

Refund policy

As we operate on a post-payment basis, no refunds are issued for classes that have already been attended.

Schedule availability

Class schedules are subject to change. We will notify you in advance of any adjustments.

Code of conduct

Respect for mentors and fellow students is mandatory. Disruptive or disrespectful behaviour may result in suspension from classes.

Account security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account and login credentials.

Policy updates

We may update these policies from time to time. You will be notified via email and on the platform of any significant changes.

Support

For any issues or queries, please contact our support team at alexaonlineclasses@gmail.com.
