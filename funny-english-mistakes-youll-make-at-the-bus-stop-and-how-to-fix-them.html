Funny English Mistakes You’ll Make at the Bus Stop (and How to Fix Them!)

Nov 30, 2024

The bus stop—an everyday setting that turns into a comedy show when we try to speak English. Whether you’re chatting with a friend or asking someone about the bus, the mistakes can be hilarious. And trust me, if you're a college student or a young professional, you're bound to make a few funny blunders in Hinglish! Let’s break down some of the most common (and funny) errors you might make while waiting for the bus.

1. “Is The Bus Coming Now?” – As If the Bus is a Time Traveler!

You’re waiting for the bus, and you’re so eager to get home that you ask, “Is the bus coming now?”—as if it’s time-traveling through the space-time continuum! You're probably expecting the bus to answer back like a superhero.

Funny Example: “Is the bus coming now?” Your friend might reply, “If the bus could talk, I think it would say, ‘Chill, yaar, I’m not your servant!’” 😂

Fix: “Is the bus here yet?” Short, sweet, and to the point!

2. “When The Bus Will Come?” – A Grammar Disaster in the Making

You’re in a rush, and you ask, “When the bus will come?” Well, sorry, but that’s not how you’re supposed to arrange your words. If the bus had feelings, it would be offended!

Funny Example: “When the bus will come?” Your friend chuckles and says, “The bus is not a Bollywood movie with suspense, it’s just a bus!” 😂

Fix: “When will the bus come?” It’s a question, not a Bollywood plot!

3. “The Bus Don’t Stop Here” – Bus or Grammar Police?

You see a bus zipping past the stop, and you complain, “The bus don’t stop here.” Well, the bus may not stop, but your grammar is definitely not stopping either—and that's a problem!

Funny Example: “The bus don’t stop here.” Your friend might say, “Looks like your grammar is busier than the bus itself!” 😂

Fix: “The bus doesn’t stop here.” Keep it simple—grammar matters too!

4. “How Much Time the Bus Will Take?” – A Busy Bus, Busy Mistake!

You’re running late, and you ask, “How much time the bus will take?” While you're calculating your fate, the English part of your sentence seems lost in translation!

Funny Example: “How much time the bus will take?” Your friend laughs, “Are you asking the bus to become a time machine?” 😂

Fix: “How much time will the bus take?” Because time travel is not on the schedule.

5. “The Bus is Not Coming Fast” – What’s With the Hurry?

You’re waiting for the bus, and you mutter, “The bus is not coming fast.” It’s almost like you’re asking the bus to hurry up like it's running a marathon!

Funny Example: “The bus is not coming fast.” Your friend says, “The bus is not a race car, buddy!” 😂

Fix: “The bus is taking longer than expected.” Keep the complaints to a minimum!

6. “This Bus Goes To Station?” – Are You Sure It’s Not Going to Mars?

You see a bus approaching, but you're not sure if it's going to the right place. So you ask, “This bus goes to station?” As if it’s some cosmic question about the universe!

Funny Example: “This bus goes to station?” Your friend replies, “I think you’re asking if the bus goes to Mars!” 😂

Fix: “Does this bus go to the station?” Simple and clear!

7. “I’m Late for Bus, Can You Help Me?” – Who Are You Asking? A Superhero?

You see the bus coming, and you panic, “I’m late for bus, can you help me?” Well, unless your friend has super speed, they might not be able to help!

Funny Example: “I’m late for bus, can you help me?” Your friend laughs and says, “If I had superpowers, I’d just teleport you to the bus!” 😂

Fix: “I’m late for the bus, can you help me catch it?” No superheroes needed!

8. “Is This Bus Going To My College?” – The Bus Doesn’t Read Your Mind!

You’re standing at the bus stop, and you ask, “Is this bus going to my college?” The bus isn’t a mind reader, and neither is your friend, so it’s not that easy to know!

Funny Example: “Is this bus going to my college?” Your friend says, “The bus is not your personal chauffeur, yaar!” 😂

Fix: “Does this bus go to my college?” Asking the right question makes all the difference!

9. “Bus Is Late, I Am Waiting For So Long” – Bus or Your Patience?

You’ve been waiting forever, and you say, “Bus is late, I am waiting for so long.” Well, your patience might be more late than the bus itself!

Funny Example: “Bus is late, I am waiting for so long.” Your friend says, “Are you waiting for the bus or are you waiting for a miracle?” 😂

Fix: “The bus is late, I’ve been waiting for so long.” Patience is key, my friend!

Conclusion: Wait for the Bus, But Don’t Wait for Mistakes!

So, the next time you’re at the bus stop, just remember these hilarious English mistakes and the simple fixes that go with them. Whether you’re waiting for the bus or chatting with a friend, your English can make the wait a little more entertaining (and grammatically correct)! So, take a deep breath, keep it chill, and remember, no matter what—the bus is always on time when you're having fun!
